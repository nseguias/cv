The falling hex characters on each side of this CV are the real Bitcoin mempool — actual unconfirmed transactions waiting to be mined.

On load, ~1,000 transaction hashes are fetched from the blockchain. A live WebSocket streams new ones as they arrive. When a block is mined, confirmed columns flash green and fade.

Hover a column to freeze it. Click it to see full transaction details — sender, recipient, amount, fee — fetched live from the chain.

Speed, brightness, and position are derived from each transaction hash — same mempool, same visual.
